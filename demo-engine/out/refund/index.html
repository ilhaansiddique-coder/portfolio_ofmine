Refund Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Your satisfaction is our top priority. If you are not satisfied with your order, our refund process is simple and transparent.
1

Refund Eligibility

You are eligible for a refund if the product is wrong or defective, damaged in transit, or significantly different from its description.

2

Refund Process

Contact our support team to start a refund. Share your order number and a short description of the issue, with photos if applicable.

3

Refund Timeline

Once approved, refunds are issued to your original payment method, typically within 5–7 business days.

4

Exchange Policy

Prefer an exchange? We will replace the item with the same product, subject to availability, at no extra cost.

5

Contact

For any questions about refunds or exchanges, visit our contact page — we are always happy to help.
