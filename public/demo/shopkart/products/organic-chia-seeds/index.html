Product Details

Discover the Organic Chia Seeds harvested from sustainably grown chia plants and cleaned without heat or chemical treatment, leaving every tiny seed whole and full of life; this seed is 100% natural, unprocessed, and organic—preserving all of nature's goodness.

Rich in plant-based omega-3, soluble fibre, and complete protein, Organic Chia Seeds are more than just a topping—they're a tiny powerhouse of everyday nutrition.
