How to Order

From browsing to doorstep in five simple steps.

1. Find a product

Browse the home feed, categories, or search for what you want. Tap any card to open the product details.

2. Choose to buy solo or group

Tap “Buy now” to purchase at the solo price, or “Start group buy” to unlock the lower group price by teaming up with other shoppers.

3. Add to cart & review

Open the cart drawer to review your items, adjust quantities, and see your total savings before checkout.

4. Check out

Confirm your shipping address, pick a payment method, and place your order. Payment is taken securely at this step.

5. Track your order

Follow your order status under My Account → My Orders, from “Group buying” through to “To receive.”

Tips for a smooth group buy

  • Share your group link with friends to fill it faster.
  • If a group doesn't fill in time, your order auto-cancels with a full refund.
  • Watch for the “Super Deal” and “Lightning Deals” badges for the deepest discounts.
